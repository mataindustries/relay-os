# RelayOS founding-pilot playbook

## Who to target

Prioritize an owner-led home-service business with one identifiable Office Manager / Dispatcher role, recurring owner interruptions, accessible source material, and an owner willing to review policy. The best first client values delegation and traceability more than a broad software feature list.

Avoid prospects who currently require multi-company tenancy, protected public access, regulated-data handling, durable multi-user audit, automatic integrations, or guaranteed financial outcomes. Phase 4 does not supply those capabilities.

## Hiring-trigger outreach

Use a specific trigger such as an active office-manager/dispatcher job post, a recent hire, an owner stepping away from dispatch, a second location increasing call volume, or an experienced employee leaving. Do not claim knowledge of private staffing problems.

Suggested message:

> I help home-service owners transfer one Office Manager / Dispatcher role as reviewed source-backed guidance, authority, and escalation—not just an SOP folder. I’m offering a hands-on founding pilot. If that role is currently being hired or handed off, would a 20-minute scope conversation be useful?

## Discovery call

Confirm:

1. Which one role is in scope?
2. Which repeated questions or decisions return to the owner?
3. What source material exists today?
4. Which authority limits and escalations need explicit review?
5. Who will act as owner approver?
6. How much interview/review time is available?
7. What information must stay out of the current static application?
8. What qualitative outcome would make the pilot useful?

Do not promise savings or completion before reviewing the source and decision scope.

## Scope boundary

Choose either:

- **Founding Pilot — $750:** one role, up to eight core procedures or decision areas, authority/escalation map, deterministic question workspace, two weeks of guided refinement, final gap report.
- **Role Transfer Sprint — $1,250:** one role, owner knowledge capture, source organization, up to twelve core procedures or decision areas, authority/escalation map, employee question workspace, first 30-day onboarding structure, gap report, final Role Transfer Report, and four weeks of guided refinement.

Record exclusions: authentication, production persistence, integrations, billing, uploads, automated model work, multi-role scope, and guaranteed outcomes.

## Source collection

Use [CLIENT_INTAKE_QUESTIONS.md](CLIENT_INTAKE_QUESTIONS.md). Inventory job descriptions, current SOPs, policies, checklists, customer scripts, dispatch notes, and owner decision notes. In a public deployment, use only fictional or non-sensitive material. Real client sources belong in a controlled private working environment until production controls exist.

## Owner interview

Work one prioritized gap at a time. Retain the owner’s exact answer as evidence, but keep the resulting claim visibly unapproved. Clarify numeric limits, currencies, destinations, urgency, and exceptions as structured decisions rather than parsing them from prose.

## Review process

For each candidate:

1. Verify company/role/topic scope.
2. Confirm exact source references.
3. Resolve explicit conflicts.
4. Verify sensitive and owner-only boundaries.
5. Review candidate wording.
6. Record an explicit approve or reject decision with a reason.
7. Confirm only current approved knowledge appears in the employee workspace and manual.

## Employee walkthrough

Demonstrate one eligible cited answer, one above-authority escalation, one prohibition or withhold case, the sensitivity warning, and how to submit a corrected question. Make clear that topic and context selections are deliberate inputs, not automatic interpretation.

## Delivery

Use [DELIVERY_CHECKLIST.md](DELIVERY_CHECKLIST.md). Print/save the actual-record Role Transfer Report and approved-only Operating Manual summary. Download the default-minimized JSON handoff; include source text only after explicit owner confirmation. Store deliverables through the separately agreed controlled delivery channel. RelayOS Phase 4 has no import.

## Case-study request

After the agreed review period, ask whether the client will complete [CASE_STUDY_TEMPLATE.md](CASE_STUDY_TEMPLATE.md). Offer anonymous publication. Require approval for facts, metrics, quote, screenshots, attribution, and channels. “Not measured” is an acceptable result; never manufacture a before/after outcome.

## Upsell path

First offer a defined refinement extension for additional reviewed topics within the same one-role boundary. A future production deployment may be considered only after paid-pilot evidence justifies a separate security, identity, authorization, and persistence plan. Do not sell a feature that the current repository does not provide.

## Exact post-Phase-4 sales action

Use `/pilot` and the seven-minute fictional `/demo` to book one paid **$750 Founding Pilot this month** with a home-service owner who is hiring or onboarding an Office Manager / Dispatcher; complete the intake checklist before accepting real material and conduct client work only in a controlled private environment.
