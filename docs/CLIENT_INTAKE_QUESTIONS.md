# RelayOS client intake questions

Use these questions to scope one Home-Service Office Manager / Dispatcher role. Record sensitive answers only in the agreed controlled private process; the public static deployment is for fictional or non-sensitive data.

## Target role and business overview

1. What is the exact target role title?
2. Who currently performs the role, and who will receive it?
3. What is the role’s mission in one sentence?
4. What home services does the company provide, in what service area and timezone?
5. Is there a current job description? Who last reviewed it?

## Recurring responsibilities

6. What outcomes must this role own daily, weekly, monthly, and per service call?
7. What evidence shows each responsibility was completed correctly?
8. Which responsibilities generate the most repeated owner questions?
9. Which exceptions consume the most owner time?

## Sources and scripts

10. Which SOPs, policies, checklists, dispatch notes, and owner notes are current?
11. Which customer scripts are approved for intake, scheduling, delays, complaints, estimates, payments, refunds, or cancellations?
12. Which source versions are known to be obsolete or conflicting?
13. Who can confirm source authenticity and current applicability?

## Authority limits

14. What may the employee decide without approval?
15. Which decisions have numeric limits? Record the amount, currency, and exact subject.
16. Which exceptions always require approval?
17. Which actions are prohibited?
18. What customer commitments may or may not be made?

## Escalation contacts

19. Which triggers must be escalated immediately, same day, or routinely?
20. What explicit destination owns each escalation?
21. What minimum context does that person need?
22. What response should the employee expect while waiting?
23. What happens when the primary destination is unavailable?

## Sensitive information boundaries

24. Which customer, employee, credential, payment, health/safety, legal, or regulatory information must stay outside RelayOS Phase 4?
25. Which sources or approval notes are owner-only?
26. What controlled private environment and delivery channel will be used for real client work?
27. Who can authorize source-text inclusion in the JSON handoff?

## Current tools

28. Which dispatch, CRM, phone, accounting, messaging, calendar, or document tools does the role use today?
29. Which tool steps need documentation, without asking RelayOS to integrate with those tools in this phase?

## Desired outcomes and availability

30. What qualitative change would make the role transfer useful?
31. Is there a measured baseline for owner interruptions? If not, record “not measured.”
32. How much owner interview and review time is available each week?
33. Which employee can participate in the walkthrough?
34. What date should the first review occur outside RelayOS?
35. Which requested outcomes are explicitly outside this pilot?
