# RelayOS seven-minute sales demo

Use only the fixed fictional Summit Comfort Heating & Air workspace in a public demo. Open `/pilot`, then enter `/demo`. Do not enter or expose real client information. The script is a conversation guide, not a claim of guaranteed savings, compliance, revenue, or employee performance.

## 0:00–0:45 — Owner problem (45 seconds)

**Show:** `/pilot`, “The owner problem.”

**Say:** “A job description lists duties, but it rarely captures the decisions that still interrupt the owner: what is approved, what limit applies, and when the employee must stop. RelayOS packages one role as reviewed knowledge, authority, and escalation—not just a folder of instructions.”

**Ask:** “Which operational role still sends the same questions back to you?”

## 0:45–1:45 — Approved knowledge principle (60 seconds)

**Show:** `/demo`, steps 1–2, then `/sources` if useful.

**Say:** “Source material does not become company policy merely because it was pasted or extracted. RelayOS keeps the source, candidate claim, and owner decision separate. Only a current source-backed claim with an exact approval decision can enter an employee answer.”

**Point out:** The fictional label, source version/reference, claim lifecycle, and approval record.

## 1:45–3:15 — Employee question and cited answer (90 seconds)

**Show:** The within-limit discount outcome in `/demo`, then `/employee`.

**Say:** “The fictional dispatcher selects Discounts, a financial action, no sensitive data, and USD 75. The approved guidance and structured USD 100 authority boundary both match, so the fixed policy firewall delivers a cited answer. The source and owner approval remain visible.”

**Point out:** Explicit topic, typed amount/currency, approved claim, exact source, exact decision, and absence of a knowledge gap.

## 3:15–4:45 — Escalation and policy firewall (90 seconds)

**Show:** The USD 125 discount outcome and `/escalations`.

**Say:** “The same question above the recorded limit cannot receive permission. RelayOS routes the request to the recorded fictional owner and preserves the gate trace. It does not parse prose for a different limit, invent a recipient, or treat a one-time resolution as policy.”

Then show the technician-late commitment example: “Approved guidance supports a revised-window update, but an exact-arrival promise exceeds the structured boundary and routes to the service manager. No unsupported promise is generated.”

## 4:45–5:45 — Gap improvement loop (60 seconds)

**Show:** `/owner` coverage, a genuine open gap, and links to `/interview` or `/review`.

**Say:** “A missing, conflicting, invalid, unsupported, or unclear-authority condition can create a genuine gap linked to the question. A known approval, emergency, prohibition, or escalation rule does not create a fake gap. The owner improves the system through source-backed review and a separate approval.”

## 5:45–6:30 — Report and manual (45 seconds)

**Show:** `/report` and `/manual` print controls.

**Say:** “The delivery package uses actual session records. The Role Transfer Report shows the role, coverage, gaps, examples, dependencies, and provenance. The manual summary organizes only employee-visible approved guidance; it does not invent Phase 5 procedure steps.”

Mention the minimized JSON handoff and that Phase 4 has no import or durable persistence.

## 6:30–7:00 — Founding pilot offer (30 seconds)

**Show:** `/pilot`, offer and CTA.

**Say:** “The $750 Founding Pilot covers one role, up to eight core procedure or decision areas, an authority and escalation map, the deterministic question workspace, two weeks of guided refinement, and a final gap report. The $1,250 Role Transfer Sprint expands that to twelve areas, a 30-day onboarding structure, a final Role Transfer Report, and four weeks of refinement. Which role should we scope first?”

## Required closing disclosure

RelayOS Phase 4 is an unauthenticated, session-only delivery tool and deterministic demonstration. Use public routes only with fictional or non-sensitive data. Real client work requires a controlled private process until production security and persistence have their own approved phase.
